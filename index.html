Free to learn, by design

Built to remove barriers to medical learning.

This library is a starting point, not a substitute for clinical judgement. It brings authoritative, legally free resources into one quiet place — so more time goes into reading, questioning and practising.

  • 01

    Free at the source

    Every title is available at no charge from its publisher or an official open repository.

  • 02

    Linked with care

    Each destination opens the publisher's own page — no mirrors or paywalls. A few optional platforms may ask for a free account.

  • 03

    Made for study

    Chosen for clear teaching, clinical relevance and usefulness to students in Somaliland.
